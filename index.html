Standard protocols
Deworming rotation
Rotate every 4–6 weeks: Albendazole 10% → Levamisole HCl → Ivermectin. Dose strictly by body weight. Observe 21-day withdrawal period before slaughter.
Vaccination schedule
Entry: Clostridial + CCPP + Blantrax. Booster 4 weeks after primary. Annual boosters thereafter. Maintain cold chain 2–8°C. Record batch number on every event.
Ectoparasite control
Amitraz (Triatix) every 6–8 weeks. Weekly tick and lice checks during morning health rounds. Treat Lot 15 animals before reintroduction to production lots.
