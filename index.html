Standard protocols
Deworming rotation
Rotate every 4–6 weeks: Albendazole 10% → Levamisole HCl → Ivermectin. Dose strictly by body weight immediately after weighing. Never estimate — under-dosing drives anthelmintic resistance. Treat Lot 15 separately before reintroduction.
Vaccination schedule
Entry: Clostridium perfringens D (pulpy kidney) + Pasteurellosis (Mannheimia haemolytica). Booster at 4 weeks. Annual boosters thereafter. Maintain cold chain 2–8°C. Record batch no. and expiry on every event card.
Ectoparasite control
Amitraz (Triatix) every 6–8 weeks. Rotate annually with Cypermethrin. Weekly tick and lice checks during morning health rounds. Animals in Lot 15 must be treated before moving back to production lots.
